FAQ

Common questions

Do you work with small businesses or only enterprises?

Both. Our background is enterprise data and AI, and we package that same expertise into solutions like AI assistants, smart websites and dashboards that growing businesses can launch in weeks.

Where should we start if we're new to data and AI?

With a short discovery call. We'll look at your goals and current tools and recommend one or two practical first steps with clear value.

How long does a project take?

Ready-made solutions typically launch in 1–4 weeks. Platform and enterprise projects are scoped individually, usually in phases.

Does your AI understand Arabic?

Yes. Our AI solutions handle Arabic (including Gulf dialects) and English, and switch automatically based on the user.

Who owns the data and what you build?

You do. We build on open standards and set everything up in your accounts, with no lock-in.
